Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Mandouby uses Google sign-in only on the coordinator's own Google account, to upload class material (lecture slides, sheets) into the class Google Drive folders and to store backups of the bot's own data in that account's Drive.

  • Google user data accessed: Google Drive files and folders of the signed-in coordinator account, only to create, rename and move class material and backups.
  • The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored on the bot's private server only and can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.

Mandouby's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
